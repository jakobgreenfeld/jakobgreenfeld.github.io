I built three more sites that publish counts and nothing else.

The first one, [Podcastquery](https://podcastquery.com/), loads the public index of podcast feeds and reports how many there are.

I assumed the headline number would be the number of podcasts, and that most of them would be at least occasionally alive.

There are 4,711,475 feeds, and [437,693 of them published an episode in the last 90 days](https://podcastquery.com/research/how-many-podcasts-are-there).

892,068 feeds have exactly one episode and never got a second.

So the nearly-five-million figure people quote is mostly a count of sign-ups on hosting platforms.

I hit the same problem on [ProfessionLens](https://professionlens.com/), which holds state licence rolls for doctors, agents, engineers and a few other licensed jobs.

Florida lists 972,132 licensed insurance producers, and 304,219 of them hold no active carrier appointment, so no insurer has appointed them to sell its policies.

I had been reading licence counts as headcounts of people doing the work, and in that one state the licence roll runs about a third above the appointed one.

[JournalistLabs](https://journalistlabs.com/) breaks in a different way.

It holds 127,602 editorial staff at 8,800 outlets, and every one of those rows is a job title the person wrote themselves.

My count for the New York Times comes to 128% of the 1,700 journalists the paper has published, and my BBC count comes to 72% of its 5,500 figure, which I read as former staff still listing the Times and BBC people never filling in a profile.

The ratio inside a single newsroom still held up, and it was not what I expected: the [Times lists 1,144 editors against 632 reporters](https://journalistlabs.com/research/nyt-two-editors-per-reporter-bbc-two-reporters-per-editor/), while the BBC lists it the other way round.

After three of these I now put the denominator, the source and the pull date under every number on all three sites, and I say which rows I dropped.

That is mostly so I can tell later whether a number moved or whether I just counted differently.

If you spot one that looks wrong, tell me and I will go back to the file.
